BrowsAI Third-Party License Inventory

This document is a summary of the third-party components BrowsAI depends
on and the licences they ship under. It is not authoritative; the
authoritative source is each upstream project. When redistributing
BrowsAI in source or binary form, the actual licence texts of the
components below must accompany the distribution, with their required
notices preserved.

The full list with versions is also emitted at build time by
`scripts/dump-licenses.sh` (which queries `cargo metadata` and writes
`THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.generated.md`). The generated file is the
canonical inventory; this file lists the items that warrant explicit
human review.

any derivative of Servo's source; downstream users of BrowsAI must

<https://www.mozilla.org/en-US/MPL/2.0/>

## Unicode (ICU) crates

The build pulls in the `icu_*` and related crates (18 packages) under
the Unicode-3.0 licence. The Unicode-3.0 terms require that the
copyright notice and disclaimer be retained with copies of the data
and software. When distributing BrowsAI binaries, the corresponding
Unicode notice must be preserved.

## Cryptography crates

`aead`, `cipher`, `crypto-common`, `digest`, `hmac`, `sha1`, `chacha20`,
`chacha20poly1305`, `poly1305`, `r-efi`, `subtle`, `zeroize` are used in
the credential and vault crates. They are MIT/Apache-2.0/BSD-3-Clause
respectively; their original licence texts must accompany the
distribution.

## Build and tooling crates

The build uses standard Rust tooling crates (proc-macro, syn, quote,
serde_derive, etc.). They are MIT or dual MIT/Apache-2.0 and impose no
additional attribution requirements beyond preserving the licence text.

## How this file is generated and updated

The authoritative per-package inventory with versions, repositories,
and licence strings is generated by:

```sh
cargo metadata --format-version 1 | python3 scripts/dump-licenses.py
```

which writes `THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.generated.md`. The hand-curated
`THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.md` (this file) is updated by humans when a
component needs explicit attention (licence change, attribution
addition, vendoring change). Both should be reviewed as part of the
release process.

## Notice preservation requirements (summary)

The following requirements apply when redistributing BrowsAI in source
or binary form:

- Preserve every licence text listed above with the relevant
  copyright notice;
- preserve the MPL 2.0 notice for Servo and euclid;
- preserve the Unicode-3.0 notice for ICU-derived data and software;
- do not strip copyright, licence, or attribution notices from
  vendored sources;
- do not falsely assert that BrowsAI has relicensed any of these
  components.

The BrowsAI licence does not override these third-party licences.
Any distribution that bundles these components must comply with all
applicable terms.
