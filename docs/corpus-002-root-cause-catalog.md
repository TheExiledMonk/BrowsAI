labels describe provenance, not severity:

- `KNOWN`: present in the corpus-002 baseline or its documented findings.
- `NEW`: first observed after the corpus-002 baseline.
- `REGRESSION`: a previously passing behavior broken by a later change.

`REPRODUCIBLE` means the mechanism was reproduced in a controlled fixture or
fresh site replay. `UNMEASURED` means the original-site retry still remains.
